# Fundamentals of IoT

## M2M: Machine to machine
- Direct comm btn machines
- Dont rely on internet connectivity
- Limited scalability


## IoT
- About sensors and automation
- Relies on internet/cloud access
- Data delivery depends on IP adressing
- More scalable, billions of devices can connect

## CPS: Cyber Physical System
Conprises computing devices communicating and interacting with the physical world through sensors and actuators in a feedback loop.

How physical systems can be controlled and monitored in the cyber space.


# Data collection and Signal Processing
***Data collection***: Gathering data of various quantities and transmitting from sensor node to base station.

***Signal Processing***: Analyzing and interpreting the recieved data, filtering noise and extracting relevant information.

## Causes of noise
- ***Thermal noise***: Random electrical fluctuations from the senors internal electronics. Eg small variations in temperature sensor reading even in stable reading.

- ***Quantization noise***: Errors during conversion of analog to digital eg rounding errors.

- ***Channel interference***: Signal disturbances by other wireless devices operating on the same frequency band.

- ***Environmental disturbances***: External physical factors affecting sensor readings. eg wind vibrations in structural monitoring system.

> Data fusion: Combining data from different sensor nodes in a network to produce more accurate and reliable information.
